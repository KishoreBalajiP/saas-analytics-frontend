# Cookie Policy

## Review status

This informational document describes the cookies and browser storage the application uses today. It needs legal review before it is published as final.

## Strictly necessary

- **Session cookies** (`saas_session`, `saas_admin_session`): HttpOnly cookies that keep you signed in. They are required for the service to work.

## Browser storage

- **Remembered workspace**: the workspace slug you last signed in to, stored in local storage so the sign-in form can prefill it. Use "Switch workspace" to forget it.

## Analytics and advertising

The application sets no analytics or advertising cookies.
