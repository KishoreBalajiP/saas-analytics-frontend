# Privacy Policy

## About this draft

This is a draft template. It lists the personal data the application processes today. The operator must complete the controller identity, contacts, legal bases and retention periods before publishing it as final.

## Data the application processes

- **Account data**: name, email address, password hash, workspace membership and roles.
- **Security data**: sign-in times, IP addresses and user agents of sessions, and audit records of security events.
- **Workspace content**: datasets, dashboards, reports and alerts that workspace members create or upload.
- **Consent records**: the version of these documents accepted at registration and when it was accepted.

## Your choices

- Change your name, time zone and password from your profile.
- Review and sign out your active sessions from your profile.
- Choose which notification channels you receive from the notifications page.

- Legal bases for each purpose
- Sub-processors and international transfers
